Ritreat

擴增實境眼鏡用空手做辦公，長時間雙手懸空容易帶來身體疲勞和心理負擔。Ritreat 把傳統投壺收成一段輕量、有文化根基的休息：任務告一段落時出現，引導人離開懸空姿勢，投出一箭，再回到工作，以緩解重複性勞損和心理疲勞。

Bare-hand work on AR glasses can bring physical fatigue and mental demand when both hands stay in mid-air. Ritreat turns traditional Touhu into a light, culturally grounded break: at the end of a task it guides you out of that posture, through one throw, and back to work, to ease repetitive strain and mental fatigue.

前身

前身是腕上生灵，更早的皮影原型，用 Rokid 眼鏡在辦公間隙放鬆手部、收集靈紋，並獲 Spatial Joy 2026 大灣區決賽 AR 賽道金獎，成員為劉智方、全子岳、鍾婷婷，指導教師任熹培。

Its predecessor is 腕上生灵, an earlier shadow-play prototype on Rokid glasses for relaxing the hands and collecting spirit patterns on an office break, which won gold in the AR track at Spatial Joy 2026, by Zhifang Liu, Ziyue Quan, and Tingting Zhong, advised by Xipei Ren.
