Ritreat

將傳統投壺帶入擴增實境辦公，緩解重複性勞損與心理疲勞。

Bringing Touhu into AR office work, to ease repetitive strain and mental fatigue.

項目簡介

Ritreat 是一款具文化真實度的體感遊戲。它把傳統投壺放進配戴擴增實境眼鏡的辦公工作：持續的雙手懸空操作之後，遊戲在任務間隙出現，引導人改變姿勢、拿起箭矢瞄準並投出，再回到工作。

Ritreat is a culturally authentic exergame. It adapts Touhu, a traditional Chinese throwing game, for office work with AR glasses. After sustained two-handed mid-air interaction, the game appears at a task boundary, guides a change of posture, and supports the return to work.
